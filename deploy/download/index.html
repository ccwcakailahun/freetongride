How to install on Android

  1. Tap the download button above for the app you want. Use mobile data or Wi‑Fi.
  2. Open the downloaded file from the notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, choose More details → Install anyway.
  5. Open FreeTongRide and create your account with your Sierra Leone phone number.

These are early test versions shared directly by the FreeTongRide team, so Android asks for extra permission. The apps will move to Google Play before public launch.
